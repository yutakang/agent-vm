## Purpose and tone

This material is shared in the hope that it may help people who face similar
constraints around agent autonomy, confidentiality, cost, and technological
sovereignty. Comparisons are intended to describe different threat models and
operational trade-offs. They are not intended to make unsupported criticisms of
other projects, maintainers, companies, or platforms.

References to third-party products are based on the public sources linked in
the relevant document. Products, policies, and implementations change, and the
descriptions may become incomplete or incorrect. Inclusion of a link does not
imply endorsement by its publisher, and this repository is not affiliated with
the named providers or projects unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Corrections are welcome

Factual corrections, contrary evidence, clearer threat models, and reasoned
objections are welcome through GitHub issues or pull requests. When reporting a
problem, please identify the relevant file and passage and, where possible,
include a current primary source or a reproducible technical example.

The scripts, procedures, comparisons, and recommendations are experimental and
have not received an independent security audit. They may contain mistakes,
unsafe assumptions, outdated information, or defects that cause data loss,
credential exposure, service charges, downtime, or other harm.

You are responsible for evaluating the material against your own threat model,
policies, contracts, laws, systems, and budget. Test it in a non-production,
credential-free environment before relying on it. To the extent permitted by
applicable law, the material is provided without warranty or guarantee, and you
